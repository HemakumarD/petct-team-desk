Privacy policy

This policy covers the web apps published by Hemakumar D at hemakumard.github.io, including PET-CT Desk (Apollo Cancer Centres, Vanagaram) and Lakshmi Billing. Last updated 16-Nov-2026.

What the apps store

The data you enter is stored in your browser on your own device. There is no app server, and the developer does not receive or keep a copy.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app asks for the drive.file permission only. With it, the app can create, read and update the files it made itself in your Google Drive (for example petct-team-desk-data.json), so your devices stay in step. It cannot see or change any of your other Drive files. Your Google data is used only for this sync. It is not shared, sold or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models. You can remove access at any time from myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections, and you can delete the data file from your Drive.

AI document reading (PET-CT Desk)

If you add your own Claude API key, the documents and messages you choose to read are sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API so their details can be extracted. Nothing is sent unless you start a reading.
